The Universal Control Group

  2. The 2021–2023 Rocket Launch: The moment border restrictions lifted, non-EU net migration lines detached from historical averages and went vertical.   3. The 2025–2026 Valve-Down: Provisional ONS data for the year ending December 2025 shows UK net migration plummeting to 171,000 (provisional estimate as of recent releases; subject to future revisions). Germany’s net migration (immigrants minus emigrants) fell ~45% to ~235,000 in 2025 (from ~430,000 the prior year, per Destatis). Similar tightening occurred across several EU countries.
