“What if many of today’s conflicts are not random crises, but symptoms of an older struggle between national sovereignty and imperial finance?

Trump, Globalism, Iran, China, and the New World Shift

Kokinda’s recent commentary through Promethean Action argues that the Trump movement represents more than a political comeback. It represents, in her view, a challenge to the globalist order and a possible return to the American System….

Her broadcasts and articles repeatedly connect Trump’s economic policies, tariffs, industrial revival, China strategy, Iran policy, and tensions with Britain into one broader picture: a struggle over whether America will remain trapped in a global financial system or rebuild itself as a sovereign productive republic.

This is why her insights matter for Israel.

Israel often sees the world through immediate survival: Iran, Hezbollah, Hamas, missiles, diplomacy, and American support. But Kokinda pushes the conversation deeper. She asks: Who is shaping the strategic environment behind the scenes? Who benefits from permanent war? Who benefits from weakened nation-states? Who benefits when America is deindustrialized, distracted, and divided?

Those are not small questions.”
