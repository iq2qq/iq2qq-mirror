Principal allegations include:

3. The Secrecy Whammy: Contempt Proceedings to Conceal Evidence

On January 23, 2026, Scottish Information Commissioner David Hamilton initiated unprecedented contempt proceedings against the Scottish Government after repeated failure to meet disclosure deadlines (January 15 and 22) for documents relating to the James Hamilton ethics inquiry. Those documents concern whether Sturgeon breached the ministerial code in her handling of complaints against Salmond. Ministers invoked “jigsaw identification” risks as justification; the Commissioner dismissed the argument as disingenuous delay. This marks the first occasion the Commissioner has referred governmental non-compliance to the Court of Session, exposing ministers to potential sanctions or a formal contempt finding.
