On September 29, 2025, President Donald Trump unveiled a 20-point Gaza “peace” plan at the White House, joined by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former UK Prime Minister Tony Blair as a key figurehead. Touted as a roadmap to “eternal peace,” it demands the immediate disarmament of Gaza’s de facto authorities, hostage releases, and an International Stabilisation Force (ISF) under a Trump-chaired “Board of Peace” to oversee reconstruction and governance. Leaked details expose Netanyahu’s last-minute tweaks, embedding Israeli vetoes, tying withdrawal to surrender, and securing indefinite military access via a Gaza “security perimeter”—cementing control over a devastated territory. Arab mediators are furious, unmasking the plan as a U.S.-Israeli stitch-up dressed as multilateralism. Far from a reciprocal peace process, this is a colonial blueprint that tramples Palestinian self-determination, flouts international law, and entrusts Gaza to figures with blood-stained legacies. Here’s why it’s a diplomatic sham:

A One-Sided Ultimatum: Dismantling Palestinian Agency

The plan’s 20 points, as outlined by the White House, are a study in asymmetry:

  • Immediate Ceasefire and Hostage Swap : Gaza’s authorities must free all 48 remaining hostages (20 believed alive) within 72 hours.

  • Disarmament : Complete surrender of weapons and tunnels, with amnesty for “peaceful” members and Qatar exile as a carrot.

  • ISF Deployment : An Arab-backed force (Egypt, Jordan, UAE) polices Gaza, trains a “vetted” Palestinian force, and funnels aid via UN/Red Crescent.

  • Board of Peace Governance : A Trump-Blair-led board oversees a technocratic Palestinian committee, with a vague transition to a “reformed” Palestinian Authority (PA)—if Israel approves.

  • Buffer Zones and Security Perimeter : Israel retains a Gaza-Egypt border zone and indefinite military access, gutting withdrawal promises.

  • Statehood Mirage : A “pathway to Palestinian self-determination” that Netanyahu vows to “forcibly resist.”

Gaza’s leadership is given “three or four days” to comply or face U.S.-backed Israeli escalation—a death threat, not diplomacy. Palestinian Islamic Jihad rejected it outright, citing the hostage clause as a leverage-killer without blockade relief. Even Fatah, the PA’s leading faction, offers tepid support, calling it “workable” but exposing Palestinian fractures. Gazans like Khadar Abu Kweik back it out of desperation to end a war that’s killed over 66,097 since October 2023, per Gaza’s health ministry, amid UN-confirmed famine. Journalist Fathi Sabah warns rejection risks more slaughter, but acceptance serves “Netanyahu’s interests” cloaked in peace.

This isn’t a peace process—unlike Oslo’s mutual concessions or Good Friday’s inclusive talks. It’s coercion, reducing Gaza’s 2.3 million people to pawns in a protectorate scheme.

The Rogues’ Gallery: Architects with Blood on Their Hands

The plan’s credibility crumbles under its architects’ legacies, laced with messianic fervor that frames expansionist policies as divine mandates:

  • Benjamin Netanyahu : Facing ICC arrest warrants (November 2024) for war crimes, including starvation tactics and civilian targeting, Netanyahu’s “Greater Israel” vision drives the plan’s buffer zones and vetoes. His coalition, led by Bezalel Smotrich , holds 14 Knesset seats critical to his government’s survival. On September 29, Smotrich posted on X his “red lines” for the Trump plan, demanding: “The IDF must remain permanently in the perimeter, including the Philadelphi corridor, with unrestricted freedom to operate throughout the Strip”; “No Palestinian Authority involvement in Gaza—now or ever”; “No Palestinian state on the agenda… it must be taken off the table permanently”; and for Palestinians, “Those who wish should be able to leave via Egypt and continue their lives elsewhere.” These statements, amplified widely, push for indefinite occupation, block Palestinian governance, reject statehood, and promote “voluntary” exodus—code for displacement critics call genocidal. Smotrich’s ultimatum threatens coalition collapse if Netanyahu yields, ensuring the plan serves annexationist goals.

  • Tony Blair : The Iraq War’s architect, whose 2003 invasion killed over 200,000 civilians and unleashed ISIS, now plays the “elder statesman” card to whitewash Gaza’s recolonization. Blair’s Tony Blair Institute for Global Change has pocketed millions from UAE royals and Saudi princes, peddling “reform” blueprints that sideline Palestinian voices while funneling reconstruction cash to Western contractors. His role here? Chairing the “Board of Peace” to impose technocratic rule—echoing his post-Iraq “democracy promotion” that installed puppet regimes. Trump hailed him as a "good man, very good man," but Blair's baggage from the flawed Iraq intelligence weighs heavy in Arab eyes, with critics like UN rapporteur Francesca Albanese decrying his U.S.-Israeli alignment as unfit for oversight. Former Labour leader Jeremy Corbyn blasted the appointment, noting the invasion's toll in lives and insisting Gaza's fate belongs to Palestinians, not Blair, Trump, or Netanyahu.

  • Steve Witkoff : Trump’s handpicked self-proclaimed Zionist envoy and real estate developer, this Bronx-born figure with deep Netanyahu ties is the plan’s drafting muscle. Witkoff, who golfed with Bibi and dismissed Jewish holy days to ram through hardline terms, envisions a Gaza where hostages flood out in 48 hours and “peaceful” Hamas gets amnesty—only if it bows to Israeli security vetoes. Arab voices paint him as Netanyahu’s errand boy, torpedoing any real two-state hope with buffer-zone carve-outs that lock in occupation. Fresh off meetings with Bibi’s inner circle, including Jared Kushner tag-teams, Witkoff’s blueprint reeks of real estate flips: Gaza as a securitized enclave for profit, not people. His fervor shines through in fundraising hauls—pulling in six- and seven-figure sums from Jewish backers after Biden halted arms to Israel—and his rapturous take on Netanyahu's congressional address as "strong and epic," evoking a profound spiritual resonance. Meanwhile, his son's ventures rake in Qatari millions for U.S. property deals, exposing the hypocrisy in cries against Gulf influence.

  • Mike Pompeo : Who as CIA director plotted to assassinate Julian Assange for leaking the US empire's dirty secrets via WikiLeaks. Pompeo greenlit kill squads—think London street hits or staged crashes—after Vault 7 exposed CIA hacks, all while Assange faced zero US charges. Tucker Carlson exposed Pompeo's threats over classified leaks and his push to bury JFK files, branding him a "sinister criminal" caught in the act. Now, this war criminal cheers Trump’s “remarkable” Gaza trap as a Fox News darling, having helped ink the 21-point farce with Witkoff and crew. His evangelical zeal infuses the scheme: he once vowed to Jewish leaders he'd thwart Jeremy Corbyn's rise in Britain, and proclaimed God's hand in granting Israel dominion over Judea and Samaria as the divine route to harmony for all faiths there—Jews, Muslims, Christians, Druze alike. Pompeo even mused that the Almighty might have positioned Trump, akin to Queen Esther, to shield the Jewish state from Iranian peril, affirming "the Lord is at work." His “enormous opportunity” spiel? Code for more rendition black sites in the desert, where transparency goes to die—just like Assange almost did.

Echoes of Empire: From Sykes-Picot to Trump’s Carve-Up

This plan isn’t innovation; it’s Sykes-Picot 2.0, slicing Gaza into zones of control for imperial puppeteers. Trump’s “Board of Peace” mirrors colonial mandates, with Blair’s Iraq ghosts and Pompeo’s assassin playbook ensuring no real sovereignty. Netanyahu’s vetoes and Smotrich’s exodus fantasies guarantee endless conflict, while Arab “partners” like UAE and Saudi get photo-ops for normalization bribes.

Resistance, Not Resignation

Palestinians aren’t buying this facade. From Rafah protests to Ramallah rallies, calls ring out: No peace without justice, no statehood without land. Global south voices—from South Africa’s genocide case to Indonesia’s boycotts—amplify the sham. As famine bites and bombs fall, the real path forward? Boycott the board, dismantle the buffers, and demand accountability for these autocratic rogues.
