The Fake SNP Party

Published on 22 March 2025 | Deep link: ://iq2qq.com{postSlug || ''}

The Scottish National Party (SNP), born in 1934 to shatter Scotland’s Westminster shackles, has decayed into "The Fake SNP Party"—a corrupt, gutless fraud that’s betrayed the independence dream. Since Nicola Sturgeon’s reign, through Humza Yousaf’s flop, and under John Swinney’s limp grip, the SNP has turned from liberators to parasites. Here’s why it’s a disgrace—and why it’s doomed.

Sturgeon: The Grifter Queen

Nicola Sturgeon’s decade was a con—stacked mandates squandered on hollow promises. Brexit’s Remain landslide should’ve been her weapon, but when the Supreme Court killed Holyrood’s referendum power, her “de facto” election ploy collapsed. She slunk off in 2023, shadowed by a £600k scandal—indy funds allegedly blown on party perks. Toni Giugliano, ex-SNP insider, damns her: independence faded to a “whisper” under her crooked rule. A grifter, not a leader.

Yousaf: The Hapless Hack

Humza Yousaf’s 13 months were a disgrace. His 2024 election mandate vow crashed as the SNP tanked to 9 seats from 48. Drowned in Green fallout, NHS flops, and financial rot, he offered nothing—Alba’s Ash Regan scorned his “New Scotland” papers as trash. A hapless hack, too corrupt to fake the fight.

Swinney: The Quisling

John Swinney’s reign is a surrender. He bleats for “compelling” support he won’t chase, peddling a “show-not-tell” dodge that’s pure cowardice—Salmond’s 2007 shadow minus the guts. The 2024 conference saw him lament “process” with no plan—indy’s banished to 2031, roars 100%Yes. Fergus Ewing bolted, slamming a party “no longer for Scotland” over A9/A96 deaths. Swinney’s the SNP Status Quo Party’s quisling, embalming a corpse.

Corruption’s Core

The SNP’s rotten—£600k indy cash vanished, ex-chief Peter Murrell charged with embezzlement. After 17 years, they’re down to 9 MPs, split by Swinney’s cronies and rebels like Angus MacNeil, who quit calling them “clueless.” Membership’s imploded—58,940 by December 2024, down 5,585 in six months from 64,525. Gerry Hassan tags them “exhausted”; Giugliano warns of doom without a Yes fight. Banners at 2024 skipped “independence”—a gutless confession.

Fake to the Bone

This SNP’s a fake—hoarding mandates while doing zilch. Jim Taylor rages at the stall; Dan Wood begs for focus. Alba’s referendum push? Ignored. They’re the SNP Status Quo Party—crooks clinging to devolution, not nationhood. Giugliano’s right: “We’re not here to shine Westminster’s boots.” Too bad they are.

Sinking Now

The Fake SNP’s finished—membership’s halved from 125,000, cash shriveled from £2.3m to £2m. Senior voices see 2026 as a wipeout sans a miracle Swinney can’t conjure. They freeload on Labour’s stumbles and Tory mess, but Sarwar’s Labour smells blood, and Reform UK siphons Nats. The 2024 seat crash proves it—indy’s a memory, with the movement shafted. The SNP Status Quo Party’s a sinking fraud—Scotland’s indy soul will bury it.
