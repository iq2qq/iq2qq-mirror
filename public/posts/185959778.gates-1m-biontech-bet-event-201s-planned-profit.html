Key Price and Trading Information for Gates Foundation's BioNTech Investment:

Initial Purchase: On August 30, 2019, the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation entered into agreements to purchase 3,038,674 ordinary shares of BioNTech for an initial $55 million equity investment, priced at $18.10 per share, as part of a collaboration for infectious disease research.

Stock Value Surge: By 2021, the investment's value soared to over $550 million as BioNTech's shares peaked at $447.23 on August 9, 2021, driven by the success of its mRNA COVID-19 vaccine partnership with Pfizer.

Sale and Profit: In Q3 2021 (ending September 2021), the foundation sold 86% of its BioNTech holdings, netting approximately $260 million in profit, a tenfold return on the initial investment, tax-free via foundation status.

Post-Sale Price Drop: Following the sale, BioNTech's stock price fell from its August 2021 peak of $447.23 to $153.22 by the end of 2021 (a 65% drop), and further declined to $100.42 by June 2022 (a 77% drop from peak).
