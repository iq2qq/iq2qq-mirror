Q’s Eternal Freedom: The Quantum Pulse of a New World

Published on 24 March 2025 | Deep link: ://iq2qq.com{postSlug || ''}

Yhprum fears a Brave New World dystopia, where feeds are soma, numbing us into submission (Center for Humane Technology 2023: 30% attention span drop). But Natalia Rachel’s Age of Awareness redefines soma:

“the body living in its wholeness,” “a self-guiding, self-correcting” process, “a whirling, integrated process of atoms that behaved freely and independently,” “responsive and wise.”

A PMC article on quantum phenomena speaks of “the wholeness of the physical reality,” where “seemingly separated things can be connected and can act instantaneously on each other over arbitrarily long distances.” Payal Koul’s Curated Newsletters adds: “the concept of ‘Wholeness,’” where “all matter, including individuals, are different expressions of this ‘Universal consciousness,’” a “Supreme, ‘Universal consciousness,’ as the underlying reality of our world,” per David Bohm and Vedanta.

The Quantum Mind —is a hypothesis where consciousness arises from quantum processes, like entanglement and superposition, in the brain’s smaller structures, connecting us beyond the physical (Wikipedia, Quantum Mind, 2025)...
