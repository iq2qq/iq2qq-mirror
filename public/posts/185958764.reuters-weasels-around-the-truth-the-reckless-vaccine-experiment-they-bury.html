Reuters Weasels Around the Truth: The Reckless Vaccine Experiment They Bury

"Reuters’ March 7, 2025, fact-check—“US study does not suggest long COVID is a vaccine injury”—is a slick dodge to shield a reckless novel gene therapy experiment: the COVID vaccine. They admit the Yale LISTEN study (medRxiv, Feb 18, 2025) doesn’t prove long COVID is vaccine injury. But their “no connection” line weasels past data screaming for scrutiny. With 11 peer-reviewed studies and raw signals, let’s shred their spin and expose this experimental jab’s fallout.

Systemic mRNA: The Experiment’s Smoking Gun

Reuters pitches this reckless gene therapy as a tidy shot—done and dusted. Wrong. A 2023 Lancet EBioMedicine study proves “COVID-19 vaccine mRNA spreads systemically”—blood, lymph, organs. A 2024 Vaccines paper detects mRNA in exosomes across tissues, hinting at long-term spread. The medRxiv PVS (Post-Vaccination Syndrome) study seals it: spike protein lingers in patients’ blood up to 709 days post-jab, even without infection. Normal vaccines don’t do this—S1 clears by day 14 in healthy cases. This experiment’s gone rogue. Reuters skips it. Systemic spike from this novel tech could drive chronic chaos—PVS, maybe long COVID. Weasel play—ducking the fuse on their fairy tale.

Long COVID Odds: The Experiment’s Dirty Secret

Reuters banks on long COVID’s 2020 roots to absolve this gene therapy gamble. But PLOS One (Dec 2022) hits hard: “Two doses of COVID-19 vaccination (aOR = 2.32, 95% CI: 1.17,4.58)” predict long COVID at four weeks in diagnosed cases. A 2023 BMJ Open meta-analysis links mRNA jabs to doubled myocarditis/pericarditis risk, with long COVID-like symptoms. That’s real stats—vaccines might spike risk, not shield it. Add medRxiv’s PVS-long COVID echoes—fatigue, brain fog, endless spike—and Reuters’ “no link” flops. They hush this, acting like pre-2021 erases post-2021 clues. Dodgy move: bury the secret cracking their armor.

Genetic Risks: The Experiment’s Dark Abyss

Reuters stays shallow—PVS isn’t long COVID, done. But this gene therapy’s DNA? Frontiers (2022) spots a 100% reverse match between SARS-CoV-2’s furin site and a 2016 Moderna patent—recombination whispers. Medical Hypotheses (2023) warns reverse-transcribed mRNA could hit tumor suppressor genes. Aldén (2022) proves BNT162b2 reverse transcription in liver cells in vitro. A 2023 Clinica Chimica Acta study suggests mRNA-induced DNA damage responses. Unproven? Sure. Ignorable? Not with medRxiv’s 709-day spike anomaly. Reuters won’t dive. Genetic blowback—cancer, viral mimicry—is too grim for their “safe jab” script. Weasel tactic: skirt the abyss of this reckless rollout.

Real-World Hits: The Experiment’s Body Count

Reuters concedes the study’s small scope (42 PVS cases) but shrugs off bigger red flags. Vaccines (2023) ties BNT162b2 to myocarditis worldwide—50-90% trial side effects, underreported in the wild. A 2024 Nature Communications paper shows dosing intervals cut myocarditis risk by fourfold, proving it’s a real jab hazard. X’s José Gefaell (March 2023) flags 25 highly vaccinated countries with the fattest COVID death jumps (2022-2023 vs. 2020). MedRxiv links spike persistence to PVS, some mirroring myocarditis. Spike from this experiment inflaming hearts, death spikes in jabbed nations—Reuters calls it “rare.” Weak. They dodge the body count this reckless tech might’ve racked up.

Misinfo Smokescreen: Shielding the Experiment

Reuters pins the study’s “twist” on “The Disinformation Chronicle,” branding skeptics fringe. But medRxiv muses on “overlapping mechanisms” (spike-driven?) between PVS and long COVID. Stack that with systemic mRNA, long COVID odds, genetic risks, myocarditis, death trends—it’s not tinfoil; it’s science they dodge. Ploy: slap “misinfo” on it to guard this reckless experiment from real questions.

Verdict: Reuters Unmasked

Reuters isn’t lying—the study doesn’t nail long COVID as vaccine injury. But “nothing to see” is a weasel’s dodge. Systemic mRNA, 709-day spike, 2.32 odds, genetic shadows, myocarditis, death spikes—these aren’t debunked; they’re dismissed. This COVID vaccine—a reckless novel gene therapy experiment, rushed to billions—has fallout they won’t face. They’re not debating; they’re ducking the messy truth.

Reuters hides in shadows—the data doesn’t."
