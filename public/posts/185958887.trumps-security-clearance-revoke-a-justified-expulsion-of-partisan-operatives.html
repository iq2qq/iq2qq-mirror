Trump’s Security Clearance Revoke – A Justified Expulsion of Partisan Operatives

Published on 23 March 2025 | Deep link: ://iq2qq.com{postSlug || ''}

Donald Trump’s decision to revoke security clearances from Kamala Harris, Hillary Clinton, and a roster of critics is a necessary action against partisan operatives who have exploited their positions to undermine him. This purge targets former officials Fiona Hill and Alexander Vindman from the 2019 impeachment debacle, GOP defectors Liz Cheney and Adam Kinzinger, Judge Letitia James of New York, and 51 intelligence officials linked to the Hunter Biden laptop deception.

The Offenders: A Network of Deceivers

The BBC (March 22, 2025) outlines Trump’s list: Harris, Clinton, and Biden allies like Blinken, Sullivan, and Monaco lose access, alongside Hill and Vindman, who fueled the baseless Ukraine impeachment. Cheney and Kinzinger, who backed the flawed and weaponized January 6th committee investigation, are included, as is Judge Letitia James and 51 former intelligence officials who misrepresented Hunter Biden’s laptop. .

Vindman’s involvement is alarming. In 2019, he testified that the omission of “Burisma” from Trump’s Ukraine call transcript was insignificant (PBS, Nov 19, 2019). Social media posts (@myman5005, 2019) allege he facilitated covert arms deals through Ukraine and Burisma, later admitting to fabricating testimony. The Washington Times (2021) also ties him to Ukrainian intelligence and leaks to anti-Trump whistleblowers, cementing his role in a discredited impeachment effort. Vindman prioritized falsehoods over American patriotism, making his clearance loss a clear necessity. Hill contributed to this narrative, with the Independent (2019) noting her testimony on Trump’s Ukraine dealings as part of a coordinated attack.

Cheney and Kinzinger abandoned their party’s values. Cheney has called Trump “cruel” (MSNBC, Oct 2024), campaigning for Harris and warning of a “dictatorship” (Guardian, Dec 2023). Kinzinger aligned with her on the January 6th panel, which PBS (2022) reports was criticized by the GOP for targeting citizens under the guise of investigation. Cheney’s 2023 book Oath and Honor accuses Trump of inciting violence, ignoring her party’s objections, further evidencing their anti-Trump bias.

Judge Letitia James: A Vendetta in Her Own Words

Judge Letitia James, New York’s Attorney General, exemplifies the misuse of judicial power for political ends. In 2018, she campaigned on a mission to dismantle Trump, labeling him an “illegitimate president” who “colluded with foreign entities” and engaged in “a consistent pattern of laundering money from foreign governments in New York.” She declared his “time in power is nearing its end,” emphasizing that “the fundamental act of voting” was the key to his downfall (@kimmagagal2, Oct 2, 2023). Her statements reveal a deep-seated vendetta, exposing her bias as she pursued Trump, including a 2022 fraud case against his business (Washington Times, 2021). Social media (@stillgray, 2024) highlights her contradiction—claiming impartiality while her words scream otherwise. Her judicial role demands neutrality, yet her actions mark her as unfit for sensitive access.

Clinton’s Dossier, Harris’ Role, and the Intelligence Community’s Deception: A Corrupt Network

Clinton’s clearance revocation stems from the Steele dossier, a 2016 smear campaign funded by her team and the DNC through Perkins Coie and Fusion GPS (BBC, Oct 2017). The dossier falsely alleged Trump cavorted with Moscow hookers and spies, a narrative wildly amplified by mainstream media outlets like CNN and BuzzFeed, et al, which published the unverified claims in early 2017, fueling a years-long anti-Trump frenzy. The FBI, under James Comey, knew it was baseless but concealed that key fact (NY Post, June 11, 2022). Mueller’s investigation found no evidence. Clinton’s camp paid $113,000 in FEC fines for mislabeling the expense (AP, 2022), justifying her exclusion.

Harris, as Biden’s VP, contributed to the anti-Trump narrative, claiming in 2020 that he was “compromised” by Russia without evidence (Politico, 2020). Her involvement in an administration that protected Hunter Biden while attacking Trump ties her to this corrupt network.

The “Spies Who Lie” group—51 ex-intelligence officials from across the U.S. intelligence community, including CIA contractors—falsely labeled Hunter Biden’s laptop as Russian disinformation in 2020 (House Judiciary, 2025; NY Post, Jan 19, 2025). Their lie shielded Biden’s son, whose laptop revealed illicit activities, while aiming to derail Trump. Their clearance suspension is a logical consequence.

The Rationale: Safeguarding National Security

Trump cites “national interest,” and the evidence supports his decision. None of these individuals—Hill, Vindman, Cheney, Kinzinger, Clinton, James, Harris, or the 51 officials—currently hold roles requiring clearance, while their histories mark them as risks: Vindman’s Ukraine connections, Cheney’s partisan campaigning, Clinton’s dossier, James’ judicial bias, Harris’ baseless attacks, and the intelligence community’s laptop deception. The FBI’s knowledge of the Russia hoax’s falsehoods (NY Post, 2022) underscores the need for this action.

The Standard: Duty Over Deception

Trump’s move sets a clear standard. Hill and Vindman chose deception over duty; Cheney and Kinzinger opted for disloyalty; Clinton funded a grotesque smear; James abused her judicial authority; Harris perpetuated baseless claims; the 51 officials lied to protect Biden. Allowing these actors access invites further damage—Vindman’s alleged arms deals, James’ legal crusade, and the laptop cover-up highlight the danger. Trump avoided such mass revocations in his first term, but now, with a reinforced mandate, he acts decisively. The nation requires a leader who upholds allegiance, not one tolerating deceivers. This revocation is a vital safeguard.
