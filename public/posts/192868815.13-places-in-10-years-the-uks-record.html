13 Places in 10 Years! The UK's Record-Low Corruption Score

Published on 1 April 2026 | Deep link: ://iq2qq.com{postSlug || ''}

https://www.transparency.org.uk/news/corruption-perceptions-index-2025-uk-corruption-concerns-risk-becoming-new-normal

The Key Stats: A Decade of Decline

  • The UK has hit its lowest score ever of 70 out of 100.

  • Plummeting 13 places in just 10 years, falling from 7th place in 2015 to 20th place in 2025/26.

  • Dropped 12 points since its peak score of 82 in 2017.

  • The most significant drop in Western Europe, leaving it behind peers like Estonia and Ireland.

Why the Score is Plummeting

According to Transparency International UK, the decline is driven by specific systemic failures:

  • Big Money in Politics: The 2024 general election saw record-high spending, leading to a heavy reliance on wealthy donors and mega-donors who pour millions into party coffers.

  • Cash-for-Access Scandals: Reports of lobbyists and donors soliciting or receiving privileged political access in exchange for cash or donations.

  • Questionable Appointments: Allegations that major political donors have received government appointments without following due process or proper vetting.

  • The Mandelson-Epstein Controversy: Ongoing investigations into

    Peter Mandelson for misconduct in public office regarding his close ties to Jeffrey Epstein and the leaking of sensitive information.

  • Cronyism: Transparency International cites the ennoblement of party donors and the revolving door between public and private sectors as key drivers of public distrust.
