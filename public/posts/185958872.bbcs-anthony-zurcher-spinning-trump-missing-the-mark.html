BBC’s Anthony Zurcher: Spinning Trump, Missing the Mark

Published on 21 March 2025 | Deep link: ://iq2qq.com{postSlug || ''}

https://www.bbc.co.uk/newsletters/zgmn46f

Observers have long scrutinized Anthony Zurcher’s “US Politics Unspun” blog, which promises to cut through political noise, yet when it comes to Donald Trump, they argue it’s been locked on one refrain since 2016: every mess signals a looming collapse. They find it laughable—nine years of this spin, and Trump hasn’t faltered. Today, he sits in the White House, re-elected in a stonking 2024 victory, defying Zurcher’s narrative and the mainstream media’s (MSM) relentless witch hunt, often ignoring exculpatory evidence. Critics also point to Trump’s political censorship, alleging collusion between social media platforms and the Biden administration, which silenced him after January 6. Here’s how Zurcher’s blog kept forecasting ruin—from Russia to the Capitol insurrection—while missing Trump’s staying power.

The 2016 Kickoff: Chaos as Collapse

From the start, Zurcher’s 2016 post-election analyses—like those on Trump’s “chaotic transition”—cast every misstep as a sign of impending doom. Detractors say it set the template: Trump’s disorder meant he couldn’t last. History proved otherwise—he did, and the spin persisted.

Russia Rhapsody: Mueller’s Big Bust

Between 2017 and 2019, Zurcher’s blog hyped the Russia probe. His January 2019 piece, “A big fuse is lit,” epitomized the hype: “Washington has become a political powder keg” after Buzzfeed claimed Trump directed his attorney to lie to Congress. Zurcher wrote, “… this would appear to present a strong case for presidential obstruction of justice,” hinting Mueller held the match. Critics scoff—the report found no collusion, Buzzfeed’s story crumbled, and Trump stood firm. Spin outran reality.

Impeachment Double Feature: Nails That Missed the Coffin

In 2020, Zurcher’s coverage of the Ukraine scandal painted it as a slow bleed—each hearing a step toward collapse. The Senate acquitted. Then, in 2021, the January 6 insurrection became his next “defining moment.” His blog framed the Capitol attack—where Trump supporters stormed the building, disrupted the 2020 election certification, and left five dead in the broader chaos—as a potential endgame. Another acquittal followed. Trump marched on, unbowed.

Insurrection Fallout: Overblowing January 6, Ignoring Trump’s Peace Call

The January 6, 2021, insurrection handed Zurcher a prime chance to spin collapse. His pieces—like “Trump impeachment trial: What verdict means”—cast the riot, with its pipe bombs, assaulted officers (140 injured), and breached Capitol, as Trump’s political obituary. He leaned on the House’s impeachment for “incitement of insurrection,” citing Trump’s earlier Ellipse speech urging supporters to “fight like hell.” Yet, Zurcher and the MSM largely ignored Trump’s 2:13 p.m. tweet that day:

Critics argue this omission skewed the narrative, painting Trump as the inciter while downplaying his call for calm. By 2025, Trump pardoned nearly 1,600 tied to January 6, per Justice Department data, reinforcing his victimhood claim. Zurcher’s spin, they say, missed the mark, ignoring evidence that undercut the collapse story.

Censorship Blowback: Biden’s Alleged Collusion and Trump’s Silence

After January 6, Trump faced political censorship, with Twitter permanently suspending his account in 2021, followed by other platforms, amid allegations of collusion between social media giants and the Biden administration. Critics point to 2025 web reports—like the Washington Post’s coverage of Trump’s executive order on online censorship—suggesting Biden’s team pressured platforms to silence anti-vaccine narratives and Trump’s posts, including his peace tweets. Zurcher’s blog, they argue, never challenged this, instead reinforcing the MSM’s witch hunt narrative, further eroding his credibility as Trump rebounded to win in 2024.

Legal Drama Queens: Jail Time That Wasn’t

After Trump’s 2024 hush money conviction, Zurcher’s blog hinted at jail as a game-changer—legal peril as the next collapse. An unconditional discharge in January 2025 proved otherwise; days later, Trump took office. Observers see another overblown miss.

Election Oops: Scandals Don’t Scare Voters

Heading into 2024, Zurcher’s pieces flagged scandals—like January 6’s lingering taint—as voter repellents. Polls wavered, GOP doubts surfaced. Trump’s landslide win silenced that. Observers note the “looming collapse” spin ignored his base’s loyalty, even amid censorship claims.

2025 Victory Lap: Where’s the Collapse Now?

Nine years of Zurcher spinning Trump’s chaos—Russia, impeachments, the insurrection, legal battles—into tales of imminent ruin. His 2019 “A big fuse is lit” and 2021 insurrection takes fit the mold: powder kegs that never blew, bolstered by MSM silence on Trump’s peace call and alleged Biden-era censorship. Detractors mock the repetition since 2016, amplified by a witch hunt Trump’s base saw as unfair. His second term in 2025, secured with a blowout, exposes the gap: Zurcher bet on collapse, Trump delivered defiance.

Nine years on, with Trump back in power: which of Zurcher’s recycled spins looks most absurd now?
