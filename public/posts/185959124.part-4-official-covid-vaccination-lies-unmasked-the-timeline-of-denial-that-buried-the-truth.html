Gross Medical Negligence? The Evidence Mounts

The CDC’s early 2021 stance overlooked VAERS reports exceeding 1,900 myocarditis cases by July, far above historical baselines for vaccines. This sleight-of-hand with the vaccine definition—shifting from "immunity" (pre-2021) to "protection" (post-September 2021)—allowed authorities to rebrand mRNA shots as traditional vaccines rather than experimental gene therapies, eroding public trust amid mounting evidence they didn't halt spread. WHO’s dismissal clashed with global data showing ADEM risks up to 3.78 times expected in large cohorts. The NHS’s “mild” framing in 2022 was undercut by a 2024 multicenter study of 60 vaccine-associated myocarditis cases, where 81% showed late gadolinium enhancement on cardiac MRI indicating scarring, with 60% persisting at follow-up—signaling chronic risks. The BBC’s 2023 coverage preceded the GVDN’s revelation of elevated myocarditis odds (up to 3.48 after Moderna doses in young males), data from 99 million people that amplified calls for scrutiny. FDA’s evolving warnings in 2024, amid mounting autopsy evidence, raise questions of delayed action: A 2024 systematic review of fatal cases tied vaccines to myocarditis in up to 74% of autopsied sudden deaths post-vaccination. Is this willful blindness or calculated cover-up?

A September 2021 Guardian piece acknowledged myocarditis risks in teen boys but downplayed severity compared to COVID, citing experts who viewed cases as transient—before 2024 autopsies revealed otherwise.
