The Plea: What the Record Now Confirms

Under a deal reached with federal prosecutors in Maryland, Morens admitted to a deliberate scheme to subvert the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and hide federal records concerning the origins of COVID-19 and terminated coronavirus grants.

Key admissions from the court filings include:

  • The Gmail Workaround: Morens and his co-conspirators used personal Gmail accounts to pass along nonpublic NIH information and "back-channel" updates to an "unidentified senior NIAID official"—whom charging documents and reporting strongly indicate is former director Anthony Fauci—to intentionally evade FOIA and public records laws.

  • The EcoHealth Connection: Charging documents and congressional investigations link “co-conspirator 1” directly to EcoHealth Alliance president Peter Daszak, whose organization funneled federal research dollars directly to the Wuhan Institute of Virology.

  • Behind-the-Scenes Shenanigans: Morens admitted to accepting illicit gratuities—including bottles of wine delivered to his home—from Daszak in exchange for his backdoor coordination and assistance in trying to restore terminated grants.
