How Starmer is exploiting cumulative deterrence to attack democracy

Cumulative Deterrence is a military doctrine used to manage non-state threats (terrorists/insurgents):

UK Government’s new Cumulative Disruption clause (Clause 140/124 of the 2026 Act) treats the British public as a threat to be deterred rather than a population with fundamental rights.

As the ICCT article explains, this strategy relies on a "consistent, long-term strategy for managing threats." Starmer is weaponising ‘the law’ to make protest inefeasible by saying that because you marched last month, you can't march this month.

His recent venerating murder insinuation is the psychological part of this coercive deterrence. He is attempting to create a guilt-by-association trap by making the cost (social shame, legal risk, being labeled a terrorist, etc). of attending a protest so high that people self-deter and stay home.
