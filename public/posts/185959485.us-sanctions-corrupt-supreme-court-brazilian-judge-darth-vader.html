Financial Times: US adds 40% tariff on Brazil and places sanctions on judge trying Bolsonaro:

“...sanctions on the nation’s supreme court judge trying former president and Donald Trump ally Jair Bolsonaro on charges of plotting a coup. De Moraes ordered Bolsonaro on July 19 to wear an ankle monitor, to observe a curfew and not to give interviews, amid fears he might abscond. Brazil’s supreme court is expected to give its verdict in August or September. Bolsonaro faces decades in jail if the top court finds him guilty...”
