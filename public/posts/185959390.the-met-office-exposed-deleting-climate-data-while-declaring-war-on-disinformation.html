The Lowestoft Scandal: Data Fabrication and the Silent Erasure

The controversy ignited with queries about Lowestoft North Parade, a weather station decommissioned around 2010. The Met Office defended its temperature records as reliable, claiming they derived from "four well-correlated nearby stations" producing monthly averages accurate to 0.1°C. Scrutiny revealed a stark truth: those stations had been closed for years, with the closest active alternatives over 35 miles distant—far too remote to justify such precision without significant error margins of up to 2°C.

Rather than address the discrepancies, the Met Office dismissed Sanders' Freedom of Information (FOI) requests as "vexatious," a bureaucratic shield against public inquiry from an entity sustained by public funds.
