1984 Zoomers: Cops Leave Live Biometric Surveillance Data Open for Bypassing Bystanders

12 August 2026

Police deployed live facial recognition and biometric scanning technology in a mobile unit at the Manchester Caribbean Carnival 2026 in Alexandra Park. The monitoring screens displaying live tracking data and crowd feeds were left completely exposed to the public.

A bystander was able to walk up, zoom in with a mobile phone camera, and capture the entire sensitive data feed on video, turning a high-tech surveillance operation into an open security farce.
