The Keiranny Framework: Guide

Tier 1: The Police State (Duty of Care Collapse)

  • This is the frontline of the Regime’s failure, where the state’s duty to protect the citizen is discarded to protect state optics.

  • It involves the weaponization of police procedure, where officers focus on narrative management. often through ideological bias, rather than providing aid to individuals in life-threatening situations, as evidenced in the Henry Nowak case.

Tier 2: Domestic Agitators (The Public Order Purge)

  • This tier focuses on the mass management of domestic dissenters, using public order laws to sanitize public spaces.

  • It treats any group that effectively mobilizes against the Regime - from ‘far-right’ activists to pro-Palestine protesters and/or critics of the State of Israel, as a singular ‘agitator’ threat that must be contained or suppressed.

Tier 3: The International Blacklist (Censorship Layer)

  • This tier serves as the state’s gatekeeper for intellectual exchange, using administrative tools like Electronic Travel Authorisations (ETAs) to pre-emptively bar foreign voices.

  • By labeling international commentators, such as Cenk Uygur and Hasan Piker, as individuals whose presence is “not conducive to the public good,” the regime effectively censors debate on its foreign policy and geopolitical alliances.

The + (Institutional Enablers/Useful Idiots)

  • This category represents the universities, media organizations, and ‘bastions of speech’ that normalized the culture of censorship long before the government codified it.

  • Institutions like the Oxford Union function as useful idiots who practice their own eleventh-hour disinvitations of speakers (e.g., Katharine Birbalsingh, Tommy Robinson, Carl Benjamin) primarily based on political pressure.

  • While these institutions now claim the state’s actions are a direct threat to free expression, they are effectively victims of a culture they helped cultivate, having paved the road for the very regime that is now suppressing their own platforms.

This structure shows that the regime is not working alone; it is operating within a framework of suppression that has been enabled by the very institutions that now find themselves caught in its gears. We are all targets of the State because the mechanism of control has now expanded to capture both the streets and the elite podiums of debate.

This machine does not distinguish between your politics - it only recognizes that you are a dissenter. By attempting to silence us all, it has inadvertently done the one thing it feared most: It has awarded the public a common enemy and, in doing so, forged the pathway to unification.
